All items in one place: the "Full lineup" grid sits right under the stage and always shows every item: price, stock, and a progress bar for anything you can't afford yet. Clicking a card tunes the monitor to it (static burst, knob turns) and the wallet recalculates. So the monitor is the detail view and the lineup is the browse view. The wallet slip shows the before/after maths, so you know exactly what you're spending before you hold.

The wallet becomes a dock. On a phone, the ticket slip collapses into a bar pinned to the bottom with your balance and "Hold to order" for whatever's on screen. Your thumb is always on the button while you scroll the lineup, and tapping a lineup row re-tunes the monitor above.

The lineup turns into compact rows (thumbnail, name, price) so all items fit on one screen. Earn and orders stack below.

The monitor stays first, the same order the Hunts tab uses on phones.

4 · Where ComfyUI earns its place

House-style item art

Right now the item images are hotlinked: a Brave image-proxy link and a Trustpilot logo, and either can disappear. Generate one image per item in a single look (think late-night infomercial product shot: the item on a turning pedestal under hard studio light, VHS grain) and ship it in public/store/. The admin keeps the image field, so new items can use generated art or any other link.

Z-Image Turbo or Flux 2 dev, about 10s per image on your 5080.

The operator

A recurring GSN host who answers the line during the order moment: standing by (idle), on the phone (connecting), and a shrug (sold out or "lines are busy" errors). Qwen Image Edit keeps one character consistent across all three poses. Optional extra: a 2-second Wan 2.2 loop of them picking up the phone, as a small webm that only loads when you actually order.

Station ident card

A GSN bumper (logo on a test-card or set backdrop) the monitor shows while the catalogue loads and when the store is empty, so those states look like dead air rather than a broken page. The GSN bug in the header can stay pure CSS.

Everything generated would live as static files in the repo. Nothing runs ComfyUI at request time.

Your call: how should "all items" work?
A

Lineup grid under the stage (shown above, recommended)

Every item is always visible without clicking anything. Cards tune the monitor. Scales fine to around 12 items.

B

On-screen guide

A "Guide" button on the bezel flips the monitor into a TV-guide grid of every item, the way you'd pull up a cable guide. Very on-theme, but everything is one click away instead of always visible, and it gets cramped on phones.

C

Both

The lineup grid for browsing, plus the guide as an extra flourish.
